Question: is the issue missing a part that its kind needs, under any heading?
- A bug needs: what happens, as observed; what should happen instead, meaning
  the behavior once it is fixed; and either steps to reproduce it or the
  evidence that it happens. A list of next steps, investigations or open
  questions is not what should happen.
- A task needs: what the work achieves, and the checks that say it is done.
A part counts wherever it is and whatever its heading, so long as it does that
job. Its kind is its label, or what it is: a problem observed, or work to do.
